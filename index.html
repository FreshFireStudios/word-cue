How detection works

Six steps.
All on the booth Mac.

A person stays between detection and the screen, unless your church turns on Auto-send.

1

Listen

Reads the soundboard feed from your USB audio interface and turns the sermon into text in real time with Apple’s on-device speech recognition.

2

Understand

Apple’s on-device model decides whether a reference just happened, including spoken addresses, ordinals like “First Timothy,” and quoted verses.

3

Check

“first timothy three sixteen” becomes 1 Timothy 3:16. The book and chapter are confirmed to exist, and quoted text is matched against the bundled KJV.

4

Filter

Anything under the confidence floor is dropped, common-word books need a chapter number, and the same verse won’t repeat within a minute.

5

Suggest

The verse appears as the Likely Match with a confidence level, waiting for your operator.

6

Send

One key and the verse is live in OpenLP through its remote connection, with keyboard shortcuts as an automatic backup.

What it understands

Preachers don’t say “colon.”

Word Cue hears scripture the way it’s actually preached and turns it into a proper reference.

“John chapter three, verse five”John 3:5
“first timothy three sixteen”1 Timothy 3:16
“verses five through seven”John 3:5–7
“…and verse eight”John 3:8
“Except a man be born of water…” BETAJohn 3:5

And it knows when you’re not quoting scripture. Books that are everyday words only count when a chapter number follows, and detection pauses during the music.

“mark my words”“acts of kindness”“a job well done”“by the numbers”“judges at the fair”

Privacy

No internet.No cloud.No recordings.

Ministers sometimes share sensitive things from the pulpit. Word Cue is built so none of it can leave the building. It uses Apple’s on-device models only.

AudioNever recorded or saved.
TranscriptsKept on the Mac for 7 days by default, or not at all.
DiagnosticsSent only when you choose, and never with audio or transcripts.
PermissionsMicrophone and Speech Recognition, used only on this Mac.
Tech specs

The details.

Platform
macOS 27 or later, Apple silicon. Native Mac app.
Audio input
Any USB or Thunderbolt audio interface, with channel selection
Speech
Apple’s on-device speech recognition, with Enhanced mode on Apple Intelligence Macs. Fully offline.
Bible
King James Version bundled and set as the default
Presentation software
OpenLP on the same Mac at launch. ProPresenter, FreeShow, EasyWorship and Proclaim are planned.
Languages
English and Spanish
Updates
Signed automatic updates that never install during a service
Price Free.

No purchase, no subscription, no per-seat cost. Install it on every church Mac you have.

The goal is missional, not financial. An optional donation supports development, and open source is planned.
