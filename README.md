# Word Cue

**Scripture on screen the moment it's preached.**

Word Cue is a Mac app for churches. It listens to the sermon through your soundboard, catches Bible references as they're spoken, like *"Acts 2:38,"* and has each verse ready to show with one keystroke.

### 👉 [Visit the Word Cue website](https://freshfirestudios.github.io/word-cue/)
See how it works, read the manual, and sign up to hear when it launches.

---

**At a glance**

- Works beside OpenLP, with more presentation apps planned
- The operator approves every verse before it goes on screen
- Speech recognition runs on your Mac, not in the cloud
- Requires an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 27 or later
- **Status:** in development
